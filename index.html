Understanding your impact

Meals wasted = Meals prepared − Meals sold

Potential people fed = Meals wasted × 2

Potential people fed is an estimate, not a count of actual donations. This version treats all unsold meals as wasted. Actual donations, stored food, and staff meals are not yet recorded separately.
